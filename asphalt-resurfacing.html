Restore instead of replace

Where the underlying structure remains suitable, resurfacing can restore an existing asphalt area without the cost and disruption of rebuilding the entire surface.

SA Asphalt repairs damaged areas, prepares the existing surface and installs a renewed asphalt layer to improve appearance and extend useful life.

The right resurfacing decision starts with understanding the condition of the existing base.

Built around the right process

Good surfacing starts with preparation, the correct materials and disciplined workmanship.

01

Evaluate the existing surface

We look for cracking, failed areas, poor drainage and base movement.

02

Repair before covering

Localised failures are addressed before a new surface is placed.

03

Renew the finish

The prepared surface receives the appropriate asphalt treatment and compaction.
