About SA Asphalt

Durban-based asphalt specialists

Through hard work, dedication and attention to detail – we have placed ourselves as one of the lead asphalting companies within the Asphalting industry. We pride ourselves in our ability to provide a professional, high quality service to our customers. Our expertise includes residential driveways, heavy duty asphalt, pot-holes, resurfacing, repairing and rejuvenating existing asphalt surfaces in the residential, industrial and commercial markets.

Our Services

During each work process, our motivated and experienced team, from the office to on site supervisors and foremen ensure that only the very best practices and workmanship are carried out using the correct grades of SABS approved materials.

  • Driveways

    Light and heavy-duty asphalting for residential, industrial and commercial markets.

  • Resurfacing

    Re-surfacing existing asphalt surfaces for residential, industrial and commercial markets.

  • Parking Lots

    We have the expertise to build car parks. We can mark roads and parking bays to your specification.

  • Drainage & Curbing

    One of the leading causes of asphalt damage is improper drainage and curbing.

  • Repairs

    Repairs to existing asphalt, as well as curbing and drainage systems.

  • Tar Shield/Rejuvenation

    Rejuvenate your existing asphalt to look brand new and add durability.

  • Concrete Works

    Building and repairs to sidewalks, concrete pathways, etc.

  • Paving

    Commercial and residential paving for driveways, patios, walkways, etc.

  • Traffic Calming

    Repairs and construction of speed humps, rumble strips and other traffic calming measures.
