Professional exterior works

Support sloped sites and protect surrounding exterior works.

Retaining walls can be an important part of stabilising level changes and creating usable space around driveways, pathways and landscaped areas. We coordinate retaining work with the surrounding earthworks, drainage and surface preparation.

  • Slope support
  • Level-change management
  • Integration with drainage
  • Exterior works coordination
Proper preparation, practical site solutions and a professional finish.
What we do

Built around the site

Every property is different. The right solution depends on levels, access, drainage, usage and how the new work connects with the surrounding area.

Site Support

Practical retaining solutions where level changes affect driveways, access routes or outdoor areas.

Drainage Integration

Water management considered alongside retaining works to reduce avoidable pressure and erosion.

Complete Exterior Works

Retaining walls can be coordinated with earthworks, paving, pathways and landscaping.

Our process

How we approach the job

1. Inspect slope & levels

Understand the site and what the finished work must achieve.

2. Plan excavation / foundation

Establish the correct preparation, set-out and working conditions.

3. Construct retaining system

Complete the core installation with attention to levels and workmanship.

4. Backfill, drain & finish

Inspect tie-ins, edges and overall finish before handover.
